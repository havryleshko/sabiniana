# Our territory (one page, 2026-10-03)

## The problem we solve
Every month a dental practice pays many bills. **Nobody checks whether they are fair, whether they went up, or when they renew.**

## Who has it
Independent UK dental practices. The decision-maker is the principal dentist or the practice manager.

## What we do each month
The practice sends its bills. We send back **one page** with four answers:
1. **What changed** since last month.
2. **Which bills are too high**, and by how much a year (illustrative, not a quote).
3. **What renews in the next 90 days.**
4. **What exactly to ask each supplier for.**

No login, no app, no dashboard. They send documents and get a finished page.

## The bills we watch (biggest first)
1. Lab work (about 6.6% of income in private practices)
2. Consumables and materials (about 7.5%)
3. Energy and premises (about 4.6%)
4. Card payment fees
5. Equipment service contracts
6. Software and phone

## Why this is ours
- It is rule-based, so AI plus plain code can do it.
- It needs no licence and is low-regulation.
- Accountants only compare costs once a year, and none of the three I checked advertise negotiating prices.
- Buying groups are free but are paid by the suppliers they steer you to. Nobody does an independent monthly check.
- It stays a service: the customer buys a finished page, not a tool.

## What is NOT our territory
| Not ours | Why |
|---|---|
| Bookkeeping, month-end reconciliation | Accountants' job; probably needs HMRC money-laundering registration |
| Associate pay statements | Same as above |
| Chasing patient payments, failed membership payments | Patient data and debt-collection rules |
| Staff costs and recruitment | Largest cost, but judgement work, not buying |
| NHS contract rules and clawback | Specialist and regulated |
| Tax | Needs qualified advisers |
| Compensation claims against suppliers or processors | Claims management is FCA-regulated |
| Billing and revenue-cycle work (coding, claims, denials) | Mostly a US market; not researched yet |

## Where we start
The card fee check, because the engine already works on the 7 samples. It is the free front door. Lab invoices come next because lab is the biggest line.

## Still to prove (hypotheses)
1. Practice owners care enough about cost creep and renewals to send their bills.
2. Typical savings are a few £k a year per practice (my sizing, no measured dental rates yet).
3. UK lab prices vary enough to matter (only US evidence found).

The public free offer is the test. We ask nobody anything.
