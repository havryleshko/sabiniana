# Engine folders

Alex drops a statement in `inbox/`. The reader writes fields into `working/`. The checker writes its result there too. The writer writes a draft into `reports/`. Alex reads every draft against the statement before anything is sent.

`reference/price_table.json` holds cited rates only. Fair all-in rates stay empty until they have a source and a date.

Customer files in `inbox/`, `working/`, and `reports/` are not committed.
