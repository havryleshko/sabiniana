# Answer key: batch of 30 sample documents (made 2026-10-04)

Everything is invented: practices, companies, numbers. Savings are illustrative, not quotes. Yearly = monthly x 12.
Benchmarks come from our research: lab prices from research/dental-open-questions.md section 3 (three UK public price lists), energy rates from MoneySuperMarket (a seller of switching, so not independent), service plan prices from The Probe.

## The mix, and why
The cost watch plan says practices send their monthly bills, with the card statement as the free front door. So the 30 are:
- 14 card statements (the front door, and the only ones engine v0 can read today)
- 7 lab invoices (the biggest, least served bill)
- 4 energy bills (easy rule-based checks)
- 3 supplies invoices
- 2 service contract documents

14 practices send more than one document, like a real monthly bundle (for example Kingsway sends a card statement, a lab invoice and an energy bill).
6 documents are fine or nearly fine on purpose. Saying "you're fine" builds trust.

## What the checker does with these (hand-typed 2026-10-04)
The 14 card statements are transcribed in tests/fixtures/card_statements.json. The other 16 documents are transcribed in tests/fixtures/other_bills.json. The checker does not open the PDFs. read() sends a PDF to Claude Sonnet 5.5 and copies the printed fields. On these 30 samples, the checker then returns the same finding codes as the hand-typed fixtures.

On a card statement it flags a flat headline rate, the top tier band, stacked small fees, PCI non-compliance, a charged rate above the agreed rate, a duplicated fee line, a total the lines do not reach, a terminal hire longer than 18 months, a minimum-monthly top-up, an acquirer margin above 0.25%, and a printed rate rise. Harbour View (#02) and Wren Street (#11) have no findings.

On a lab, energy, supplies, or service document, `check_bill` flags a line that does not multiply, a net or total the lines do not reach, the same lab case billed twice for the same item, a remake inside the lab's own free window, VAT charged on lab work (Value Added Tax Act 1994, Schedule 9, Group 7, item 2A), a courier line, a materials surcharge, a printed percentage price rise, energy that is out of contract or on a rollover tariff, a fixed energy contract ending within 90 days, an estimated meter reading, surcharge and delivery on a small supplies order, a service-plan renewal rise, and a charge for equipment the invoice marks as decommissioned. Kingsway lab (#16), Wren Street lab (#20), and Ashdown supplies (#28) have no findings.

The illustrative yearly savings in this key stay outside the checker. Lab list prices, the energy comparison of about 26p, and The Probe service-plan prices have no source in this repo, so the checker leaves them out. yearly_gap stays empty and the verdict stays incomplete.
